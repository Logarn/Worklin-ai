Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-26

This Privacy Policy explains how Worklin Social Scheduler handles data when authorized users connect social media accounts and schedule, upload, or publish content from the web dashboard.

1. Information we process

Depending on the accounts and permissions you connect, Worklin Social Scheduler may process account identifiers, display names, avatars, authorization tokens, selected media files, captions, post metadata, publishing schedules, and status information returned by connected platforms.

2. TikTok data

When you connect TikTok, the app may request access to basic profile information and content posting permissions such as user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish. This information is used only to show the connected account and to upload or publish content that you choose to send through the app.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, display account connection status, prepare posts, upload user-selected media, publish or schedule content at the user's request, troubleshoot errors, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

4. Sharing

Worklin Social Scheduler does not sell personal information. Data is shared with connected platforms only as needed to perform actions the user authorizes, such as logging in, uploading media, or publishing a post. Service providers may process data only to operate, secure, host, or maintain the application.

5. Storage and security

The service uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect account and content data. No system can guarantee absolute security, especially when third party platform APIs and user-managed accounts are involved.

6. User choices

You can disconnect a social account or revoke platform permissions from the relevant platform's account, security, or app settings. You can stop using Worklin Social Scheduler at any time.

7. Retention

Data is kept only for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, or support user-requested account and publishing workflows.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the operator of the Worklin AI application through the account or support channel used to access the service.
